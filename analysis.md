## Chart 1: Daily Threats

This chart shows phishing reports and failed logins over 60 days. The numbers change from day to day. It helps us understand when security threats happen. One limitation is that the chart does not explain why the numbers change.

## Chart 2: Weekday vs Weekend

This chart shows that phishing reports are higher on weekdays. The average is 7.98 on weekdays and 2.88 on weekends. This suggests that phishing reports happen more often during the school week. One limitation is that we do not know the reason for this difference.

## Chart 3: Login Success Rate

This histogram shows how login success rates are spread across 60 days. It helps us understand how often different success rates happen. One limitation is that the chart does not explain what causes the rates to change.
